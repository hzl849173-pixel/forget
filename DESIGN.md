# Workout Journal Design System

## Design Philosophy

The app should feel premium, modern, calm and effortless.

Users should immediately understand how to use the app.

Every screen should prioritize clarity over decoration.

---

## Visual Style

Minimal.

Clean.

Elegant.

Professional.

Avoid visual clutter.

Avoid unnecessary borders.

Avoid unnecessary shadows.

Prefer whitespace.

---

## Colors

Use a very limited color palette.

One primary accent color.

Neutral backgrounds.

High contrast text.

Avoid bright gradients unless explicitly requested.

---

## Typography

Large headings.

Readable body text.

Clear hierarchy.

Never use more than three font sizes on one screen.

---

## Cards

Rounded corners.

Comfortable padding.

Consistent spacing.

Minimal elevation.

Do not overuse borders.

---

## Buttons

Large touch targets.

Clear labels.

Consistent corner radius.

Primary actions should always stand out.

---

## Forms

Minimal fields.

Large inputs.

Large tap targets.

Avoid unnecessary confirmation dialogs.

---

## Navigation

Simple.

Obvious.

No deep navigation hierarchy.

Users should never wonder where they are.

---

## Animations

Subtle.

Fast.

Purposeful.

No flashy animations.

Animations should improve usability.

---

## Icons

Use a single icon library.

Consistent sizing.

Minimal style.

---

## Lists

Comfortable spacing.

Easy scanning.

Support searching.

Support favourites.

---

## Empty States

Always explain what the user should do next.

Never leave blank screens.

---

## Loading

Use skeleton loaders when appropriate.

Avoid blocking the user.

---

## Accessibility

Large touch targets.

Readable text.

High contrast.

Support dark mode from the beginning.

---

## Overall Goal

Every screen should feel like it belongs in the same app.

The UI should feel closer to Apple Health, Notion and Linear than to a typical student React Native project.
