# Workout Journal

## Vision

Workout Journal is a premium, minimalist workout logging application.

The goal is not to tell users how to work out.

The goal is to make recording completed workouts faster and more enjoyable than using Notes or a spreadsheet.

---

## Target Users

- Gym beginners
- Intermediate lifters
- Advanced lifters

Users already know their workout.

They simply want to record it quickly.

---

## Primary Goal

A complete workout should be logged in under 30 seconds.

Every feature should support this objective.

---

## User Flow

Launch App

↓

Google Sign-In

↓

Home Screen

↓

Choose muscle groups

↓

Select exercises

↓

Enter weight

↓

Enter sets and reps

↓

Auto-generate workout title

↓

Save

↓

View workout history

---

## Core Features

- Google Sign-In
- Exercise library
- Favourite exercises
- Custom exercises
- Search
- Multiple muscle groups
- Weight tracking
- Set tracking
- Rep tracking
- Auto workout title
- Previous workout reference
- Last 30 days history

---

## Out of Scope

Do not add:

- AI workout generation
- Diet tracking
- Calories
- Macros
- Social feed
- Messaging
- Challenges
- Community features

The app should remain focused on workout logging.

---

## Success Criteria

Users should prefer this app over using Notes because it is faster, cleaner and easier.
